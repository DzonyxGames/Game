01 / THE GAME

FIRST-PERSON INDUSTRIAL HORROR

The factory remembers.

You play as Daniel Rook, an electrician who accepts a suspicious overnight maintenance job at the abandoned Hearthline Toyworks facility.

After a violent storm blocks the road, Daniel is forced to continue on foot. Inside the factory grounds stands an old wooden house that should not exist.

Restore power, solve environmental puzzles, uncover hidden observation areas and survive MINDER — the mechanical caretaker that still believes the evaluation is running.
